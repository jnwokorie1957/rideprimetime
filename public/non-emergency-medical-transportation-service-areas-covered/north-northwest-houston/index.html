NORTH / NORTHWEST HOUSTON

Plan medical rides in North and Northwest Houston.

Arrange Houston wheelchair or ambulatory transportation around the exact north or northwest destination on your appointment notice. These medical corridors include different campuses; confirm each requested route and date with Ride Primetime.

01

Distinguish Willowbrook from other campuses

Houston Methodist lists Willowbrook Hospital at 18220 State Highway 249, Houston, TX 77070, serving northwest Houston. Use the hospital's official campus map and your department's instructions to identify the building and accessible meeting point.

02

North Houston surgery requires a campus check

Kelsey-Seybold lists its North Houston Campus ambulatory surgery location at 15655 Cypress Woods Medical Drive, Houston, TX 77014. It is a separate destination from Willowbrook. Read the campus name and address from the actual procedure instructions when discussing a ride.

03

Plan cross-town travel without a fixed-time promise

A journey from another part of Houston to the Highway 249 corridor or a north-Houston clinic needs the full pickup and destination details. Give the facility's check-in time so an outward plan can be agreed. We do not guarantee travel times or immediate availability.

04

Match recurring rides to the actual center

For dialysis or rehabilitation, confirm that each appointment uses the same address. Share the schedule, date range and exceptions, then record the dates we accept. A location on this page does not reserve a recurring slot; confirm the requested dates directly.

05

Prepare the right post-procedure handoff

Ask the surgery center who must accompany the passenger and whether that adult must remain with them afterward. A driver does not automatically satisfy a responsible-adult discharge requirement. Confirm the return exit and contact process before the appointment.

06

Ask about the whole route

Discuss boarding assistance, wheelchair fit, any companion, the outward ride and the return before agreeing to the private-pay fare. For destinations outside Houston, ask separately rather than inferring coverage from the corridor name. Facility references do not imply affiliation or a Ride Primetime branch.
