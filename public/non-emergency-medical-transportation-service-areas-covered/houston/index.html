HOUSTON

Private-pay wheelchair rides in Houston.

A Houston journey starts with two locations and a plan. Ride Primetime operates one wheelchair van, based in the 77035 area, serving Houston citywide. Route availability is confirmed separately from coverage.

01

Houston-wide trip planning

We accept trip inquiries from across Houston, including north, south, east, west and central neighborhoods. The local pages below are planning examples, not the limits of coverage. Confirm the requested date and full route directly.

02

Across-town appointments

Tell us both the pickup and destination areas, the appointment time and whether a return is needed. Confirm an agreed pickup window rather than relying on a general travel-time estimate.

03

Wheelchair fit and assistance

Discuss your wheelchair, transfer needs, any companion and the accessible pickup point directly before booking. We do not provide emergency transportation.

04

Private-pay arrangements

Request a quote for the complete journey and clarify payment and cancellation arrangements. This website does not promise Medicaid or insurance reimbursement.

05

When the finish time is uncertain

Ask how an appointment with an uncertain end time can be handled. Do not assume the van can wait or return immediately.

06

Direct contact, clear confirmation

Call 713-423-4430 or use the email link for a general inquiry. Availability is limited by a single-van schedule; a message does not create a reservation.
