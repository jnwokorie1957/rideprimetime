SOUTHWEST HOUSTON

Wheelchair transportation in southwest Houston.

Southwest Houston is part of our citywide service, near our 77035 operating base. Arrange a private-pay, non-emergency wheelchair trip by discussing the complete route and your assistance needs directly.

01

Give us the neighborhood, then the pickup details

Southwest Houston covers different neighborhoods and road corridors. When you first call, give the general neighborhood and destination area; confirm the exact accessible entrance when arranging the trip.

02

Useful local context

The City of Houston describes Central Southwest as south of South Main and west of the South Freeway, with Sims Bayou through the area. Neighborhood names help orient a request, but coverage is confirmed for the actual pickup and destination.

03

Recurring appointments from the southwest

If you need repeat trips, share each date and appointment time. Availability must be agreed for the full schedule rather than assumed from a single booking.

04

Plan for both directions

Discuss the return pickup before travel. Let us know whether you expect a fixed finish time or will need to discuss a different arrangement.

05

Equipment and pickup access

Discuss wheelchair fit, any companion and the route from your entrance to the van. Avoid assuming that steps, transfers or additional assistance are included.

06

Ask for a complete private-pay quote

Confirm the fare and any applicable waiting or return arrangements before booking. Call for availability; please keep health records out of email.
