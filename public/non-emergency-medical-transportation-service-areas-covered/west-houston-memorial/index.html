WEST HOUSTON / MEMORIAL

Wheelchair and ambulatory rides in West Houston and Memorial.

Plan a private-pay appointment ride in West Houston or the Memorial area. The useful starting point is your specific clinic and entrance, not just a hospital system name. Houston-wide coverage remains subject to route, mobility and scheduling confirmation.

01

Memorial City is not the Texas Medical Center

Memorial Hermann lists Memorial City Medical Center at 921 Gessner Road, Houston, TX 77024. Confirm the location on your appointment instructions before requesting a ride; Memorial City and the Texas Medical Center require different travel plans.

02

Choose the correct campus entrance

The Memorial City directory describes East Tower, West Pavilion and Memorial Hermann Tower entrances, plus several medical plazas. Ask your department which building and entrance to use. A campus street address is a reference point, not permission to stop at any driveway.

03

Outpatient testing and surgery

The official directory places outpatient testing and surgery parking on the west side along Frostwood Drive near West Pavilion. Confirm the actual passenger drop-off with the facility rather than assuming a parking entrance is the pickup point. Arrange any required responsible adult separately from the driver.

04

Two appointments can mean two destinations

If a specialist visit and rehabilitation or imaging appointment occur on the same day, list both addresses and times when requesting transport. Do not assume they share a building or that a second stop is included in the first fare.

05

Return from the place you will actually exit

Before travel, identify the return building and the person who will tell us the passenger is ready. Agree on arrangements if the appointment runs late. One wheelchair-accessible van serves our schedule; a west-Houston pickup is not an immediate-dispatch guarantee.

06

Bring a complete request

Call with the pickup area, facility building, date, check-in time, mobility aids or wheelchair, companion and return needs. Ask for the private-pay quote and cancellation terms. We do not claim a local branch, hospital contract or medical supervision.
