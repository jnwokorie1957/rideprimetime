HOUSTON SERVICE AREA

Transportation across Houston.

Ride Primetime serves Houston with private-pay wheelchair and ambulatory transportation for planned, non-emergency trips. Citywide coverage and a confirmed reservation are different: each route, date and assistance need must fit the available schedule.

01

Start anywhere in Houston

Ask about pickups in north, south, east, west and central Houston, including southwest and southeast neighborhoods. Tell us both ends of the journey; our service is not limited to the three areas featured on the older website.

02

Medical Center and cross-town trips

For a Texas Medical Center visit, identify the building, department and requested arrival time. For appointments elsewhere in Houston, use the same complete-route planning process. Travel and return arrangements are agreed individually.

03

Confirm your trip arrangements

We review timing, wheelchair fit, boarding assistance and any companion before accepting a trip. Citywide service does not mean immediate dispatch, unlimited capacity or guaranteed pickup.

04

Bellaire and surrounding communities

Bellaire remains included in our service information. For a location outside Houston, call with the full route before making plans. A place mentioned in a historical coverage list is not a confirmed reservation.
