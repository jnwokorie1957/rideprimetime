REQUEST AVAILABILITY

Let’s plan your ride.

Call or email to discuss availability and a private-pay quote. A message is an inquiry; it is not a confirmed reservation.

01

Start with a call

Have your Houston pickup area, destination building, preferred date and required check-in time ready. Tell us whether you need wheelchair or ambulatory transportation, any mobility aid and any accompanying passenger. For recurring dialysis or rehabilitation, list the requested dates.

02

Confirm the details

Confirm the route, pickup window, fare and cancellation terms directly. Discuss who will call when you are ready for the return and how timing changes are handled. For outpatient surgery, arrange any facility-required responsible adult separately; a driver does not automatically meet that requirement.

03

Keep sensitive details off email

Please do not email medical records, insurance numbers or detailed health information. Call to discuss the appropriate next step.
