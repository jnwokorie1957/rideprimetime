AMBULATORY TRANSPORTATION

Ambulatory rides for Houston appointments.

Private-pay, non-emergency transportation for passengers who can travel seated and whose boarding and assistance needs fit our service. Plan a Houston clinic visit or day-surgery journey directly with Ride Primetime.

01

Describe how you will board

Tell us whether you walk independently or use a cane, walker or other mobility aid. Discuss steps at the pickup point, assistance needs, seating and any companion before booking. Being ambulatory does not by itself establish that a particular trip is suitable.

02

Book the arrival and return together

Use the facility's check-in time when requesting the outward trip. Ask how a variable discharge time can be handled, who will contact us and whether waiting is included in the quote. Confirm any waiting arrangements and the return pickup before travel.

03

A ride is not a discharge escort

For a procedure involving sedation, ask the surgery center what it requires of a responsible adult, including accompaniment and care after arrival home. A driver does not automatically meet that requirement. Arrange the required adult separately and confirm the plan with the facility.

04

Confirm suitability after a procedure

Follow the facility's discharge instructions and let its team decide whether ordinary seated transportation is appropriate. Ride Primetime does not provide medical monitoring or replace emergency transportation. Do not use an online inquiry as discharge approval.

05

Ask for your private-pay quote

Call with the date, pickup and destination areas, arrival time, mobility needs and return plan. Confirm the fare and cancellation terms directly. Please do not email medical records or insurance numbers.
