REHABILITATION APPOINTMENTS

Keep rehabilitation rides on the calendar.

Discuss Houston transportation for physical therapy, occupational therapy and other scheduled rehabilitation visits. Plan wheelchair or ambulatory travel around the actual clinic location and accepted appointment dates.

01

Confirm the outpatient location

A rehabilitation provider may operate at several sites. Use the location on the appointment confirmation, not just the health-system name. TIRR Memorial Hermann describes both inpatient and outpatient care; confirm the specific outpatient clinic with its scheduling team.

02

Bring the appointment series

Share the days, check-in times, expected visit length and date range. Identify any reassessment visits with different times. We review each requested ride against the requested schedule before confirming recurring transportation.

03

Discuss access and mobility

Tell us about wheelchair fit, mobility aids, boarding assistance and any companion. Ask the clinic about access from its drop-off point to the therapy area. If assistance needs change during the course of therapy, review the travel plan before the next trip.

04

Coordinate changes early

Let us know if the clinic moves an appointment, adds a session or changes the location. Ask how cancellations and waiting affect the fare. A series of appointments does not create an automatic standing reservation.

05

Transportation supports the visit

Your rehabilitation team sets treatment and clinical instructions. Ride Primetime provides non-emergency transportation, not therapy, medical supervision or emergency transfer. Confirm private-pay pricing and the return arrangement directly.
