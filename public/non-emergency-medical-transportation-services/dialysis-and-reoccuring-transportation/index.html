DIALYSIS & RECURRING APPOINTMENTS

Plan dialysis and recurring rides in Houston.

Discuss private-pay wheelchair or ambulatory transportation for a repeating Houston appointment schedule. Each date and return trip needs confirmation; one accepted ride does not reserve a recurring slot.

01

Build a complete weekly request

Have the center name, pickup area, required arrival time, days of the week and first and last dates ready. Share holiday changes and appointments that run on a different schedule. We review the series for scheduling and trip suitability.

02

Coordinate the return with the center

Ask the center how it communicates that a passenger is ready to leave. Agree on a pickup location and contact process before travel. A treatment schedule is not a guaranteed finish time, and an immediate return cannot be assumed.

03

Plan for changing mobility needs

Discuss whether the passenger travels in a wheelchair or walks with assistance, along with any companion. If needs change, contact us before the next ride so suitability can be reviewed. Clinical questions and treatment decisions belong with the care team.

04

Confirm dates, fares and changes

Request a quote for the proposed schedule and ask how cancellations, missed visits, waiting and rescheduling are handled. Keep a record of the dates actually accepted. This is private-pay transportation; insurance or Medicaid reimbursement is not promised.

05

Keep a workable backup plan

Ask the center or your support person about alternatives if a trip cannot be accepted or the schedule changes. Confirm the full route and requested schedule before booking. Call 911 for a medical emergency.
