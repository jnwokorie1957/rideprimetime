HOSPITAL OUTPATIENT TRANSPORTATION

Plan the ride around your hospital visit.

Arrange Houston wheelchair or ambulatory transportation for a planned hospital outpatient visit. Building access and return coordination matter as much as the street address.

01

Identify the actual destination

Confirm the building name, street address, department, floor or suite and approved drop-off entrance with the hospital. A large campus may have several entrances; a general hospital name is not enough to agree on a meeting point.

02

Use the check-in time

Discuss when the hospital wants you to arrive, rather than relying only on the procedure time. Ask about wheelchair access between the entrance and department and arrange any needed assistance inside the building separately.

03

Coordinate release and return

Ask the department who can confirm that you are ready to leave and which exit to use. Discuss an uncertain finish time before booking. Our driver is not a clinical escort and does not determine whether you are ready for discharge.

04

Confirm the whole journey

Agree on pickup, return, companion arrangements and private-pay pricing. If the hospital requires a responsible adult after sedation, arrange that person separately; booking a driver does not automatically satisfy the requirement.
