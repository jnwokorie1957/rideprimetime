OUTPATIENT & DAY SURGERY

A practical plan for outpatient and day-surgery rides.

Plan private-pay transportation to Houston outpatient clinics and ambulatory surgery centers. Discuss ambulatory or wheelchair needs, the facility's arrival instructions and the trip home before confirming.

01

Before booking a day-surgery ride

Ask the center for its check-in time, exact entrance and transportation or escort requirements. Tell Ride Primetime about boarding needs and any adult traveling with you. The center must approve your discharge arrangement; transportation alone does not guarantee acceptance.

02

Separate the driver from the responsible adult

A driver does not automatically satisfy a surgery center's responsible-adult discharge requirement. Ask whether your adult must check in, remain on site, accompany you home or stay with you afterward. Arrange the required person separately and follow the center's instructions.

03

Use the correct campus

Kelsey-Seybold lists several ambulatory surgery campuses, so confirm the exact campus on your appointment instructions. Its directory lists Bay Area ASC as opening in 2027; do not treat it as a currently open surgery destination.

04

Agree on a variable return

Procedure and recovery times can change. Discuss who will call when you are ready, the pickup point and how waiting or rescheduling affects the quote. Confirm the return pickup plan in advance, including how to handle a changing procedure end time.

05

For appointments without sedation

Clinic reviews, imaging and other outpatient visits still need an agreed arrival and return plan. Confirm mobility and access needs directly. Keep medical records out of email and use the facility for clinical instructions.
